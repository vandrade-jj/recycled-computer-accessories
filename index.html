SDG 12 • Responsible Consumption and Production

Recycled Computer Accessories

A social enterprise idea for customizable keyboards, mice, headsets, and desk mats made from recycled and repurposed materials.

Problem

What social/environmental issue is most meaningful?

Computer accessories may seem small, but they can create a lot of waste over time. Keyboards, mice, headsets, desk mats, packaging, wires, and electronic parts are often replaced when people want a newer design, better setup, or upgraded features.

When these products are constantly replaced, the materials can contribute to plastic waste and electronic waste. This connects to SDG 12 because the goal is to rethink how products are made, used, and replaced.

Product/Service

What business idea can help solve the problem?

My product idea is a line of computer accessories made from recycled and repurposed materials. This would include keyboards, mice, headset parts, and customizable pieces made from recycled plastic.

Desk mats could be made from reclaimed or repurposed fabric. Customers could also personalize parts like mouse shells, keyboard keycaps, and replaceable panels without needing to replace the entire product.

Customer

Who is buying your product?

My ideal customer would be tech-focused students, gamers, remote workers, and desk setup enthusiasts, mainly between the ages of 16 and 35.

They spend a lot of time using computers for school, work, gaming, or content creation, and they are likely to shop online for accessories. Their persona is the eco-conscious tech user who wants stylish, customizable computer accessories that are better for the environment.

Marketing

How are you getting your product in front of your audience?

The marketing strategy would focus heavily on online platforms where tech users already spend time. This includes TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, gaming communities, desk setup pages, and tech review content.

The brand could partner with small tech creators, sustainability influencers, and desk setup pages to show the product in real use. The product would be sold through a simple website and promoted with short videos showing customization, recycled materials, and setup transformations.

Branding

How will you connect with your customer?

The brand identity would feel modern, clean, and eco-tech. It should connect with customers who care about their setup looking good, but also want to make better choices with the products they buy.

The vibe would be futuristic but practical: dark colors, recycled material textures, teal or green accents, and a focus on customization. The message would be that sustainability does not have to look boring or low-quality.

Impact

How is the world better because of your work?

This business would give waste materials a second life by turning recycled plastic and reclaimed fabric into products people use every day. It would also encourage customers to think differently about tech accessories and the cycle of constant replacement.

The impact is not only reducing waste, but also showing that sustainable products can still be stylish, customizable, useful, and high quality.
